Welcome to Pokeanki! Use this Anki add-on to incorporate the Pokemon game into studying!
Choose a starter Pokemon to get started, and evolve your starter pokemon as you complete flashcards.
![pokedex][./images/game1.png]

When wild Pokemon appear, throw Pokeballs at them by answering flashcards. Equip different pokemon to evolve them. Happy Pokemon catching!
