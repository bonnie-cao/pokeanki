### Welcome to Pokeanki!<br>
Use this Anki add-on to incorporate the Pokemon game into studying!  
Choose a starter Pokemon to get started, and evolve your starter pokemon as you complete flashcards. <br>
![pokedex](./images/game1.png)<br>
<br>
<br>
### Catch and evolve!<br>
When wild Pokemon appear, throw Pokeballs at them by answering flashcards. Equip different pokemon to evolve them.  
Happy Pokemon catching!<br>
